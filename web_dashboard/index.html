Alteryx workflow (.yxmd) -- authored, not yet run in Designer

Tools 1-4

Input Ingestion

Four Input Data tools read the HCP master, monthly Rx, CRM call log and territory roster CSVs.

Tools 5-6

Aggregation & Formula

Summarize aggregates longitudinal TRx/NRx claims. Formula computes Brand Share % and NRx Adoption Index.

Tool 7

Tile Tool (Deciles 1-10)

Ranks prescribers into 10 equal-record buckets on market TRx (decile 10 = top 10%).

Tools 8-11

SFE Targeting & Joins

Joins HCP master with claims & CRM logs. Formula classifies HCPs into Tiers 1-4 and computes call deficits.

Tools 12-16

Territory Sizing & Output

Summarizes workload vs. rep capacity. Flags over- and under-utilized territories. Writes the HCP and territory marts.
